- **The Board's columns fold, so what's your move stays in sight.** Click a column's title to fold it to a narrow
  strip, which shows its count, in a pill when something in it needs you, and click the strip to open it again;
  BriefBar remembers. A column with no cards folds by itself until a card arrives. Where the window is too narrow, as
  with bigger text, BriefBar folds what you need least first, **Merged**, **Waits** and **To start**, then the columns
  where you wait on others, and keeps **In progress** and the columns where it's your move open, before it scrolls: in
  a 946-point window, the Board shows three open columns and the rest as strips, rather than cutting off the fourth.
  Right-click a title or strip for why it's folded, and **Let BriefBar Decide**. While you drag a card, a folded column
  springs open as you hold the card over it, and **⌘→** and **⌘←** move a card into a folded column too. Merged, which
  showed only its count until you clicked it, opens by itself when there's room.
