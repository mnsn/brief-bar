- **Set as next in another mission once the first lets it go.** Where two missions' orders have a pull request you set as
  next to merge in one, the Board sets it as next in the other once nothing still holds it in the first: once you moved
  what it waited for there out of that mission, or put that one after it with Merge After. It said it would still wait
  there.
- **A stage names an order as you rename its mission.** Where two orders put pull requests the other way round, the
  stage that says to check the order names the other as its mission's header does as soon as you rename it, give it
  BriefBar's name back, or Apple Intelligence names it, rather than once BriefBar next reads your pull requests.
