- **Move your work on My Work's Board to set its state.** With **Make Changes in Azure DevOps** on, drag a task, story
  or bug of yours in the sprint to another column on My Work's Board, or press **⌘→** or **⌘←** on its card, and
  BriefBar sets its state in Azure DevOps, as you, so that your team sees your work there too: **To start** sets it
  **New**, **In progress**, or any column after it up to **Waits**, **Active**, **Merged** **Resolved**, where its type
  has that state, and **Completed** **Closed**, or the states your team's process has instead, such as **To Do**, **In
  Progress** and **Done**. Its card goes to its new column once Azure DevOps has it, the line under the header says what
  changed, with **Undo**, and its right-click menu has **State** too. Your work outside the sprint says to move it there
  first.
- **The keyboard stays on the card you move.** On the Board, a card that **⌘→** or **⌘←** moves keeps the keyboard in its
  new column, so that the next key moves it on, though another card comes after it, which used to take it.
