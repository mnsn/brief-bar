## Descriptions

Azure DevOps: only with My Work Page, while My Work shows + Sprint Tasks with Show Links on, your team's current
sprint, as while its Sprint section is on screen, and the pull requests linked to its work items.

After this update: only with My Work Page on, Azure DevOps reads your sprint, and the pull requests linked to its work
items, while My Work shows your sprint tasks too.
