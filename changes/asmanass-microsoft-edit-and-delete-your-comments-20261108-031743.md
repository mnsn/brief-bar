- **An edit doesn't replace a newer version of your comment unseen.** If a comment you're editing changes meanwhile, in
  Azure DevOps or another window, your edit now says so, with what the comment says now, and **Save** saves nothing:
  **Start Over** begins again from what it says now, and **Save Anyway** replaces it with your edit. Opened again, your
  edit keeps what you wrote, and says the same, rather than dropping it without a word.
- **Esc keeps your edit.** Esc, or the edit's new **✕**, closes it and keeps what you wrote, as in a reply, until you
  save it; only **Cancel** forgets it.
- **Nothing substitutes what you write.** Smart quotes and dashes, text replacement and grammar checking stay off in
  your reply and your edit, as autocorrection does, even when your Mac's settings for them change while one is open.
