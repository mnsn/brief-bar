- **Choose how high My Work's Map shows parents.** **Parents**, at the top of the Map, is now a menu: **Stories**, as it
  starts, shows your tasks' stories; **Features** their features too, then each of your team's backlogs above, by the
  names it gives them, such as **Epics**, or **Key Results** and **Objectives** where it has those; **All** goes on to the
  top of each chain; and **None** leaves parents out, as it stays if you'd unchecked Parents. BriefBar remembers what you
  choose.
- **Others' parents stay off My Work's Map until you check Others' work.** A parent that's someone else's, or no one's,
  showed whatever Others' work said. Now it shows only with Others' work on, faded and outlined dashed, and counts with
  the rest of others' work; without it, the line from your work goes on up past it, to the next parent that shows.
