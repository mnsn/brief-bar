- **Split a mission that became two pieces of work, in one click.** When a mission's pull requests look like
  different work, Apple Intelligence suggests splitting it, on your Mac, with each new mission's name and what goes
  where. **Split** does it, and Undo puts it back; **Keep Together** keeps it from asking again until the mission
  changes.

## Descriptions

Azure DevOps: with Pull Request Order on, while Apple Intelligence is on, a split suggested on this Mac for a mission
whose pull requests look like different pieces of work, from the same titles its name is, which moves nothing until
you click Split; with the suggestions you keep together kept, each as digests of what its mission is made of, never a
title.

Copilot: with Pull Request Order on, while Apple Intelligence is on, the titles of the sessions on a mission's pull
requests also help Apple's on-device model tell whether they look like different pieces of work, to suggest splitting
it, on this Mac.

After this update: while Apple Intelligence is on, the titles of Azure DevOps' pull requests, work items and stories,
and of the Copilot sessions on them, also suggest splitting a mission that became different pieces of work, on this
Mac; nothing moves until you click Split. BriefBar keeps only which suggestions you kept together, as digests of
references.
